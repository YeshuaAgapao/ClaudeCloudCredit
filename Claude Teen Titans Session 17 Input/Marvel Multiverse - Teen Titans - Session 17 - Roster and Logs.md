## **Campaign Notes** {#campaign-notes}

* On an earth that is very similar to Earth-16, the Young-Justice Earth  
* The Teen Titans have their own headquarters, the top four floors (plus the roof) of a 30-story skyscraper.in Bludhaven.  
* The Iron-Side first-encounter was on the 4th full day of the campaign.  
* Mostly for **LLM AI Consumption** (such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) for producing high-verisimilitude in-character reports / letters / memos.  
  * Iron-Side ‘official’ role is to fulfill compulsory education requirements of the younger members of Bludhaven Teen Titans.  
  * Session 0 to mid-session 10  
    * This GM has a large pattern of the adults either being dead (in one X-Men campaign), gone / off-world, inattentive, distant, or being just the 'friendly uncle' that provide fun but do not provide any discipline and/or letting the kids tag along on missions that the responsible adults would never permit (Wolverine and deadpool in a X-Men game)..  
    * In this Teen Titans game, it is more the mainstream superheroic adults are just gone, in deep space (mainline Justice League), and the rest of superheroic adults are unattentive, and with the exception of one superheroic adult, who is distant (Nightwing), remote-mentoring.  
    * The Justice League is gone, in space, and may not ever come back (if the GM follows some of his X-Men campaign trends)  
    * Nightwing is extremely busy picking up as much of the slack of the absent Justice League as he can, at least in the Gotham / Bludhaven area, and maybe more, possibly up to the entire northeast corridor (Boston to Washington DC). He does not have time to directly supervise or in-person mentor the team.  
  * Mid-Session 10:  
    * The Justice League has returned. There have been some casualties, most of them fairly quickly restorable. Batman is one of the casualties that were not quickly restorable, having sustained a lasting serious injury and is unable to fight, and Nightwing has to take his place for a decent period of time. Iron-Side’s role remains unchanged. Nightwing has more availability now, and more support has become available.  
    * It appears that Batman was the only long-term casualty, and there are no permanent & complete casualties (deaths). The other Justice League casualties appear to have been short-term. Though there has been no on-screen interaction or communication with Martian Manhunter, Superman, Aquaman, Hal Jordan, Dr Fate, or Zatanna between sessions 10 and 16, so their long-term health is unknown.

**Ad-Hoc GM Rulings And House Rules**

* A GM house rule allows adding levels of sturdy to physical defenses (melee, agility, resilience) and levels of uncanny to mental defenses (vigilance, ego, logic).  
* If a logic or ego based power is capable of exerting physical strength, then logic (telekinesis) or ego (elemental control for some types) can substitute in place of melee in relevant checks such as escaping grabs.  
* Having both telekinetic / elemental protection and more than 2 levels sturdy at the same time is banned.  
* Can only concentrate on one instance of a power at a time (can’t maintain two barriers or two grabs, but can maintain one of each)  
  * This is actually the rules as written in the Marvel Multiverse books  
* Reactions restore at the start of a character’s turn.  
* Quasar-Force is not getting any positive or negative special treatment for his 4’11” height and 84 lbs weight, thus he is considered average size.  
* Intimidate makes the target operate as if here demoralized / zero-focus for one round  
* Damage multiplier bonuses provided by weapons do not stack with damage multipliers granted by powers (brilliant / discipline / mighty / accuracy).  
* Damage reduction provided by armor do not stack with damage reduction granted by powers (sturdy / uncanny)

## **DMReckless Marvel Multiverse House Rules** {#dmreckless-marvel-multiverse-house-rules}

**From the official house rules PDF:** 

**Automatic Fantastic**  
Any power that gives you an automatic Fantastic Result with powers that grant bonus attacks on a Fantastic result on a power only grant a single bonus attack.  After the first bonus attack, they instead give you an edge on the Marvel die of additional bonus attacks.

**Banned Powers**  
Multiversal Travel Powers, Narrative Powers, Swap Status, Time Powers

**Edges**  
When building characters, you may not get more than two automatic edges with a specific attack or power, and extra edges will have to come from situational things like Karma, Statuses, etc. rather than "every time I use this attack I have 3 edges". So, for example, if you have extra limbs and blazing   
fast fists, you won't be able to take a Trait or Power that gives you an edge on a melee attack. Or if you've got blazing fast fists and a melee attack that grants an edge, you wouldn't be able to have that as a signature attack as well. 

**Elemental Powers**  
When setting up a character with Elemental Powers, choose either Agility or Ego to use the powers.  If you choose Agility, any power that uses Ego uses Agility instead, and vice versa if you choose Ego.  Elemental powers are the only ones designed to be heavily multi-ability-dependent and this removes that. 

**Iconic Items**  
Iconic items require GM approval.  In order to get that, they should be built without access to powers you couldn't get at your Rank+1, shouldn’t have powers from more than two power sets, and should have no more than two Restrictions/Power point reductions. Items that are grabbing universally useful powers, a bunch of free powers outside your power set, gain "the best" powers without prerequisites, etc. will likely not be approved by the GM. 

**Phasing**  
When phasing, you cannot be affected by most things that do (physical) Health Damage. House-Ruled exceptions to this include powers using the Force element. Force Fields also prevent you from phasing through them. (Force Energy and Force Fields use the vectors between molecules ("energy without matter") to cause disruption or prevent intrusion.) Additionally, you can be affected by any Phasing-based attacks or by the melee attacks of other phasing individuals.  Mental attacks affect you normally, and magical abilities might affect you as well, if they create mystical rather than physical effects. Additionally, the maximum damage multiplier you can get for phasing someone into an object with phase other is equal to your Rank \+ 4\. 

**Protection Hex**  
Rather than rolling for Protection Hex, using Protection Hex makes all attacks/effects go against your Ego Defense instead of their normal target Defense. Additionally, you gain Damage Reduction equal to your Discipline. This does not stack with other Damage Reduction. 

**Signature Attack Trait**  
Signature Attack must be chosen with each attack power you want the edge with.  If you choose a type of weapon, you just get the edge with base attacks with that weapon as a weapon. 

**Sturdy**  
Add your Ranks in Sturdy to your Melee, Agility, and Resilience Defenses.  This does not stack with any other increases to your Defenses (such as Spider Sense or Shrinking.)   

**Teleport Other**  
The maximum damage multiplier you can get for teleporting someone into an object with teleport other is equal to your Rank \+ 4\. 

**Uncanny**  
Add your Ranks in Uncanny to your Vigilance, Ego, and Logic Defenses. This does not stack with any other increases to your Defenses.

The Narrator should give short and direct answers without detailed descriptions. It is perfectly fine for the answer to be cryptic and to leave the player with more questions than they started with.

  These answers do not dictate what is fated to happen. They only give hints as to possible futures. The choices the players make and the chances they take have a tremendous impact upon these outcomes.

  Terribly unlikely things happen all the time, which means that the character’s sense of the future is not infallible. However, they are rarely outright wrong.

  This is a great power for a character the Narrator controls. They can use it to lead the players around, give them guidance and perhaps even set them up for a horrible betrayal. The characters, of course, don’t have to believe what a precognitive character tells them and might even openly defy their advice.

  However, this can be an incredibly challenging power to handle as a player. The Narrator shouldn’t spoon-feed details to the player. They should answer the player’s questions in generalities when possible and not offer extra details. If the player isn’t concerned enough about something to ask about it, then it doesn’t occur to the character to consider that particular aspect of the future either.

  If appropriate, the Narrator can state that the future is too uncertain to be determined at any particular moment. This often happens at climactic moments when there are too many possible futures to consider or when the ones available are equally likely to happen. The Narrator should use this option judiciously, though. If the character can’t ever effectively use their power, that’s a sure road to frustration.

  The Narrator can also present the character with visions that the character didn’t ask for. These often happen when something vitally important is destined (or at least likely) to occur that can negatively affect the character or people they care about. This could be a personal crisis or a disaster that’s bound to catch the character up in it. Such visions still cost Focus. They drain the character, even if they were not requested.  
* Temporal Range by Level: Level 1 24 Hours, Level 2 1 Week, Level 3 1 Month, Level 4 1 Year

### **Lift / Carry Limits** {#lift-/-carry-limits}

**Powers:**

* Mighty \- Physical Lifting, Melee  
* Brilliant \- Lifting via Telekinisis, Logic  
* Uncanny \- Lifting via Elemental Control, Ego

A character can carry anything their own size or smaller on the Objects & Sizes table, and they can also swing or throw it. With a Challenging Melee check, they can lift something one size larger than they normally can, but they cannot carry, swing or throw it.

Certain traits and powers can affect this. For every level a hero has in the Mighty power, for instance, they are treated as one size bigger for these strength-related actions.

Exemples:

* An average sized character with Mighty 2 can lift / carry a truck without a check, or an airliner with a challenging melee check  
* An average sized character with Brilliant 4 with Telekinetic powers can telekinetically  lift / carry a cruise ship without a check, or a skyscraper with a challenging logic check.

A challenging target number is Rank \+ 10\.  
A rank 4 character with logic 7 and brilliant 4 will succeed a challenging check with a 7+ on 3D6 in combat or a 3+ on 3D6 outside of combat.
